# Ballistics Speed Micro Examples

Comparing the **slowest**, **average**, and **fastest** bullet velocities from the dataset in multiple units.

---

## 🐌 SLOWEST: 300 Blackout 208 gr. A-MAX®

**Muzzle Velocity:**
- **1,020.00 fps** (feet per second)
- **310.90 m/s** (meters per second)
- **31,089.60 cm/s** (centimeters per second)
- **310,896.00 mm/s** (millimeters per second)
- **0.310896 mm/μs** (millimeters per microsecond)

**Context:**
- Travels **310.90 meters** in 1 second
- Travels **0.31 millimeters** in 1 microsecond (1/1,000,000 second)
- Speed: **Mach 0.91** (subsonic - slower than sound)

**Distance Traveled:**
- In 1 millisecond: **31.09 cm** (about 1 foot)
- In 1 microsecond: **310.896 μm** (about the width of 3 human hairs)
- In 1 nanosecond: **0.31 μm** (smaller than a bacterial cell)

## 📐 Conversion Reference

### Distance Conversions
- 1 foot = **0.3048** meters
- 1 foot = **30.48** centimeters
- 1 foot = **304.8** millimeters

### Time Conversions
- 1 second = **1,000** milliseconds (ms)
- 1 second = **1,000,000** microseconds (μs)
- 1 second = **1,000,000,000** nanoseconds (ns)

### Speed of Sound
- At sea level: **≈ 343 m/s** (1,125 fps)
- **Subsonic:** < Mach 1.0
- **Supersonic:** Mach 1.0 - 5.0
- **Hypersonic:** > Mach 5.0

## 🎯 Fun Facts

1. The **fastest bullet** (4,450 fps) could travel:
   - From home plate to the outfield fence (400 feet) in just **0.09 seconds**
   - The length of a football field (100 yards) in **0.068 seconds**
   - One mile in **1.18 seconds**

2. The **slowest bullet** (1,020 fps) is still traveling at:
   - **695 mph** - faster than a commercial jetliner
   - **Just below** the speed of sound (Mach 0.91)
   - Still **extremely dangerous** despite being "slow"

3. The **average bullet** (2,777 fps):
   - Travels nearly **half a mile** per second
   - Would cross Manhattan (13.4 miles) in about **26 seconds**
   - Is traveling at **1,893 mph** - faster than a rifle bullet fired in 1800s

4. In the time it takes to blink (**~150 milliseconds**):
   - The slowest bullet travels **46.6 feet**
   - The average bullet travels **127 meters** (416 feet)
   - The fastest bullet travels **203 meters** (667 feet)
