mode                 msgs      sec       msg/s     MB/s  vs floor   stdout B
nim-ingest        300,000    2.785     107,701      6.6      100%          0

NOTES
==============================================================================
* 'nim-ingest' has nothing to acknowledge with, so its rate is bounded
  by client send speed and TCP backpressure. Treat it as an UPPER BOUND
  on the floor, not an exact figure.
* Latency includes the full client->kernel->server->kernel->client loop
  on loopback. Subtract roughly 20-40us for loopback overhead to
  approximate the server-only cost.
* Every Python callback serialises on the GIL. That is inherent to
  CPython, not to pocketsocket's bridge.
* set_broadcast_mode/set_print_mode are implemented in Nim but not
  exported via {.exportpy.}, so a pure-Nim fan-out path could not be
  measured from Python.
